# Used-Car-Lot

The screaming-spokesman aesthetic. Designed to look like the print version of a local TV commercial where someone is shouting "SUNDAY SUNDAY SUNDAY" at you.

## Why it works (when it works)

This is the dominant aesthetic of high-converting local ads — car dealers, mattress stores, plumbers, roofers. It reads as "small business, real prices, real urgency." On FB/IG it works because it looks unmistakably like a *promotion*, not content. People who are in-market for the offer recognize the signal instantly.

## Color palette

Pick one combo per ad:

- Red (`#E60000`) + Yellow (`#FFD800`) + Black on white
- Red + Blue (`#003DA5`) + White (American-flag energy)
- Black + Yellow (`#FFE600`) + Red accents (warning-sign energy)

The background is usually yellow or white with a red diagonal stripe or starburst. Black does the heavy lifting for the text.

## Typography

- **Headline**: Condensed sans-serif, ultra-bold (Impact, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Anton). All caps, often italicized or slanted. 90-130pt at 1080×1080.
- **Price / number**: Same family, even bigger. The price is the visual anchor — often inside a starburst or circle. Dollar sign smaller than the number itself.
- **Body**: Sans-serif, bold. All caps for emphasis lines, mixed case for fine print.
- **Phone number**: Huge. Often as big as the headline. The phone number is the ad.

Three weights minimum: ultra-bold (headline + price), bold (body), regular (fine print).

## Distinctive elements

- **Starburst (sunburst) shape**: 12-16 point star around the price or key number. Filled in red or yellow, with the price in white or black inside. The price is the protagonist.
- **Diagonal corner banner**: A ribbon across the top-left or top-right corner with "SALE!" / "NEW!" / "LIMITED TIME!"
- **Stripe dividers**: Solid horizontal bars separating sections, often in alternating colors.
- **Heavy black outlines**: Headlines and price numbers often have a 4-8px black stroke for extra visibility.
- **Exclamation points**: Plural. Three is not too many.

## Copy voice

Direct, declarative, price-forward. Lead with the offer, follow with the proof, end with the contact info.

**Examples:**
- "$47 GUTTER CLEANING — TODAY ONLY!"
- "BRAKE PADS INSTALLED IN 30 MINUTES OR IT'S FREE"
- "WE BUY HOUSES — CASH OFFER IN 24 HOURS"

The bullets should be hard-edged, specific, comparison-friendly:
- "▸ NO CONTRACT, NO CREDIT CHECK"
- "▸ SAME-DAY SERVICE, 7 DAYS A WEEK"
- "▸ LICENSED & INSURED SINCE 2005"

The CTA is the phone number, sized big, with "CALL NOW" above it. Optionally an "@handle" or "DM SOLD" below for the FB/IG-native version.

## What to avoid

- Don't make claims the user can't back up. "INSTALLED IN 30 MINUTES OR IT'S FREE" only works if they'll actually honor it.
- Don't bury the price. The price is the ad. If the user won't share the price, push them — an ugly-ad without a price defaults to lower-converting.
- Don't use stock-photo people doing thumbs-up. Either no photo, or a real photo of the actual business/product/owner.
