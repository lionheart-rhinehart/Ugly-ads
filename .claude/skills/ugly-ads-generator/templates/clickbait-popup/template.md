# Clickbait Popup

The mobile-web-popup aesthetic. Designed to look like an ad you'd accidentally tap on a sketchy website at 2am.

## Why it works (when it works)

The visual language reads as "this is from a person, not a brand." Curiosity-gap headline + second-person framing + faux-urgency banner triggers the same scroll-stop reflex as a real popup. On FB/IG, this aesthetic punches above its weight because the feed is full of glossy brand content, and this looks like a real ad someone made.

## Color palette

Pick one combo per ad — don't mix:

- Lime (`#C4FF1E`) + Hot pink (`#FF2D87`) on white
- Electric blue (`#00B7FF`) + Yellow (`#FFD800`) on white
- Hot pink (`#FF2D87`) + Black on Yellow (`#FFE600`)

Background is usually white or yellow. The "alarming" accent is reserved for the urgency banner and CTA button.

## Typography

- **Headline**: Sans-serif, heavy weight (Impact, Anton, Arial Black). All caps, slightly slanted-italic if possible. 80-120pt at 1080×1080.
- **Subhead**: Same family, regular weight. Mixed case. Half the headline size.
- **Body / bullets**: Clean sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica). Mixed case.
- **CTA button text**: All caps, sans-serif, white on the accent color.

Mix at least two font families. The headline and CTA should feel "loud," the body should feel "informational."

## Layout (1080×1080)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚠ URGENCY BANNER (pink/red strip)  │  ← 60px tall, all caps, white text
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                     │
│   YOU WON'T BELIEVE                 │  ← Headline, 2-3 lines max
│   WHAT [audience] IS                │
│   DOING IN [city]                   │
│                                     │
│   [optional product image, ~40%]    │
│                                     │
│   ✓ specific claim                  │  ← 2-3 bullets, checkmarks
│   ✓ specific claim                  │
│   ✓ specific claim                  │
│                                     │
│   ┌─────────────────────────────┐   │
│   │  TAP HERE → GET YOURS NOW   │   │  ← CTA button, accent fill
│   └─────────────────────────────┘   │
│                                     │
│   [phone or @handle, small]         │
│   ★★★★★ "trust hack line"           │  ← optional, small
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Distinctive elements

- **Urgency banner**: Full-width strip at the top with ⚠ or 🔥 emoji and all-caps text like "TODAY ONLY" or "5 SPOTS LEFT."
- **Checkmark bullets**: Green or accent-color ✓ in front of each benefit.
- **CTA button**: Solid filled rectangle with rounded corners (8-12px radius), centered, ~70% of width. Subtle drop shadow.
- **Fake "X" close button**: Optional tiny X in the top-right corner. Don't make it look like a real OS button — keep it clearly part of the ad.

## Copy voice

Second person, curiosity-gap, slightly hyperbolic but not false. The headline should imply there's a discovery here.

**Examples:**
- "YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT MOMS IN [CITY] ARE DOING TO SAVE ON DAYCARE"
- "[CITY] HOMEOWNERS ARE FURIOUS ABOUT THIS NEW ROOFING TRICK"
- "DOG OWNERS — YOUR GROOMER IS HIDING THIS FROM YOU"

The bullets should pay off the curiosity gap with specifics:
- "✓ $47 install, no contract"
- "✓ Same-day appointments"
- "✓ Family-owned, 12 years in [neighborhood]"

## What to avoid

- Don't fake system alerts (fake iOS/Android dialogs, fake "your phone has a virus"). Those cross into deception.
- Don't make the X actually look like it closes anything.
- Don't use stock photos that scream "stock photo." Either no image, or a real product photo from the user.
